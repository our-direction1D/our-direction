KEEP THE FANDOM FUN

RULES

Short version: be funny, be creative, be respectful, and don't ruin the space for everyone else.

01

BE RESPECTFUL

No harassment, hate, bullying, threats or personal attacks.

02

NO PERSONAL INFO

Don't post addresses, phone numbers, private messages or anyone's private information.

03

POST YOUR OWN STUFF

Only upload pictures and creations you have permission to share.

04

KEEP IT APPROPRIATE

No sexual content, graphic material, spam or anything unsafe for a general fan community.

05

FACTS NEED SOURCES

If you submit a “Did You Know?”, give admins something they can actually check.

06

ADMINS HAVE FINAL SAY

Admins can edit, reject, hide or delete submissions to keep the site safe and fun.

07

COPYRIGHT & CREDITS

Only upload media you created, have permission to share, or can legally use. Always give the original source/creator credit. A credit line is not a substitute for permission or a licence.
